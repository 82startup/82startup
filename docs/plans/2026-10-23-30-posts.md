# Posts delivered as messages Implementation Plan

**Goal:** A member's post arrives as a message from them in the chat of every member they are connected with.

**Spec:** `docs/specs/2026-10-23-30-posts.md`

**Architecture:** A closed `posts` table and `messages.post_id`; `create_post` saves the post and one message per connected
member in one transaction through the existing delivery step (made rate-limit-optional); a 5 posts per 24 hours limit; a post
box on `/messages`; a **Post** label in conversations. No new dependency.

## Tasks (tests first)

1. **Database** (`supabase/tests/posts.test.sql`, `supabase/migrations/20261023160000_posts.sql`): `posts`, `post_id`, the new
   `deliver_message`, `create_post`, `my_posts`, `post_status`, `conversation_messages` with `is_post`; explicit grants.
2. **Form and action** (`src/lib/posts/form.ts`, `src/app/messages/post-actions.ts`, tests).
3. **Screens** (`post-box.tsx`, `/messages` page, **Post** label in `message-list.tsx` and the conversation page, `copy.ts`, tests).
4. **Checks and QA:** lint, unit, DB, build, tsc; browser QA with four local members, desktop and 375px.
