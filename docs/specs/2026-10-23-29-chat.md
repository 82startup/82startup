# Chat between connected members

Issue #29. Third of four threads (profile events #27 → connections #28 → chat #29 → posts as messages #30). Two members who are
connected (#28) can send each other text messages. Posts as messages (#30) will be delivered through the same delivery step.

## Scope

In: one-to-one text messages between connected members; a **Messages** page listing conversations; a conversation page; unread
marks and an unread number in the header; a **Message** button on the profile of a connected member.
Out: live updates (the page is refreshed by the member), email or phone notifications, group chats, photos, files, links turned
into buttons, editing or deleting a message, search, typing or "seen" indicators, reporting messages, admin access to messages,
posts (#30).

## Decisions (owner, 2026-10-23)

- **One-to-one text, refresh style**: a new message appears when the page is refreshed. No live connection is opened.
- Only **connected** members can chat (#28). Nobody else can send a message to a member.

## Rules

- **Who may send:** the sender and the recipient are connected at that moment. The database checks this on every send, not only the screen.
- **Disconnect or block ends the chat.** The conversation disappears from both members' lists and neither can read or send in it.
  The messages are **kept** (nothing is deleted) and come back if the two connect again. (This keeps a blocked or disconnected
  member from carrying on, without destroying history; if you prefer deleting history on disconnect, say so before approval.)
- **A message:** plain text, 1 to 2,000 characters after trimming. A message with only spaces is refused. It is shown as text with
  its line breaks, never as markup, and links are not turned into buttons.
- **Limit:** at most 30 messages per minute per sender, so a member cannot flood someone. Over the limit the send is refused with
  a clear message.
- **Unread:** a message is unread for its recipient until the recipient opens that conversation. Opening it marks everything in it
  as read.
- **No editing or deleting** in this version. Messages are deleted only when an account is deleted (they go with it).
- **Admins cannot read messages.** There is no admin screen for them.
- Messages are never sent by email, and the sender is not told whether the other member has seen them.

## What a member sees

- **Header:** a **Messages** link, with the number of unread messages when it is above zero (next to **Connections**).
- **`/messages`:** the member's conversations, most recent first: photo, name, the start of the last message (one line, cut at
  80 characters) and its time, and a mark when something is unread. Connected members with no messages yet are not listed here;
  they are reached from **Connections** or their profile. An empty list says so.
- **`/messages/[memberId]`:** the last 50 messages, oldest at the top, each with the sender's name and time; a box to write (2,000
  characters, with a counter near the limit) and a **Send** button; a **Refresh** button that reloads the conversation; **Show
  earlier messages** when there are more. The page for a member who is not connected shows "This conversation is not available".
- **Profile:** a **Message** button next to **Connected**, linking to the conversation. **Connections** lists also link to it.

## Database

One new table, closed like the connection tables (no direct read or write; everything goes through functions, `security definer`,
`set search_path = ''`, execute granted to `authenticated` only, explicit grants because production grants nothing automatically):

- **`messages`**: `id`, `sender_id` and `recipient_id` (both → `profiles`, delete cascade), `body` (text, check 1 to 2,000
  characters), `created_at`, `read_at` (null until read). A check forbids sending to yourself. An index on the pair and
  `created_at` for the conversation, and one on `recipient_id` where `read_at` is null for the unread count.

Functions:
- `deliver_message(sender, recipient, body)`: the one delivery step. Internal (not callable by members). Checks the connection with
  `are_connected` and the rate limit, trims and validates the body, inserts. `send_message` and, later, the posts feature (#30) both use it.
- `send_message(p_to, p_body)`: members. Uses the caller as sender; returns the new message id.
- `my_conversations()`: members. Connected members with at least one message: member, last message start, its time, unread count; most recent first.
- `conversation_messages(p_with, p_before, p_limit)`: members. Newest first up to the limit (max 50) before a time, for connected members only; empty otherwise.
- `mark_conversation_read(p_with)`: members. Marks the caller's received messages from that member as read.
- `unread_message_count()`: members. Unread messages from members the caller is still connected to.

## Testing (tests first)

- **Database:** a connected member can send and the other can read it; a non-connected member cannot send (and a pending or declined
  request does not count); a disconnect or a block hides the conversation, refuses sends, and a new connection brings the old
  messages back; empty, spaces-only and 2,001-character bodies are refused, 2,000 is accepted; the 31st message within a minute is
  refused and the next minute is allowed; no sending to yourself; unread counts rise on send and fall to zero on `mark_conversation_read`,
  and only for the recipient; paging with `p_before`; the list orders by last message; strangers cannot read a conversation by guessing
  an id; anon and non-members get nothing; the table is closed; `deliver_message` is not callable by members; a production-like
  grant check; deleting an account deletes its messages.
- **Unit:** the send form parsing and validation (member id, body length); the action refuses a bad form, sends a logged-out caller to
  login, words the refusals (not connected, too fast) without database text; a message is escaped; the header number; the conversation
  and list rendering.
- **Browser QA (local, two accounts):** send both ways; refresh shows the reply; unread number goes up and clears on opening; the
  list order; disconnect makes the conversation unavailable; a 2,000-character message and a long unbroken word at 375px.
- **Live:** with two real accounts after release.
