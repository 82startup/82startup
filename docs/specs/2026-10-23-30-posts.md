# A member's post, delivered to their connections as a message

Issue #30. Last of four threads (profile events #27 → connections #28 → chat #29 → posts as messages #30). A member writes a
post once and it arrives as a message from them in the chat of every member they are connected with. There is no public feed:
nothing is shown to anyone who is not a recipient.

## Scope

In: a **Post to my connections** box; delivery of one post as one message per connected member, through the existing delivery
step (#29); a **Post** label on those messages; a posts-per-day limit; the sender sees what they sent and to how many.
Out: a public or shared feed, likes, comments (people reply in the normal chat), editing or recalling a post, scheduling, photos or
links previews, posting to a chosen subset of connections, muting posts from one member, email or phone notifications, admin access to posts.

## Decisions (owner, 2026-10-23)

- The post goes to **every connected member as a direct message** (not to a feed, not to an inbox of its own).
- It is delivered **instead of** being published on LinkedIn-style feeds: the site has none.

## Rules

- **Who receives it:** the members connected with the author at the moment of posting. A snapshot: someone who connects later does not
  get it, someone who disconnected or blocked does not get it. Nobody else can see it.
- **What it is:** plain text, 1 to 2,000 characters after trimming, the same text rules as a message (no markup, links not turned into buttons).
- **Where it appears:** in each recipient's conversation with the author, as a message with a **Post** label, unread like any message,
  counted in the header number. The author sees it in each of those conversations too (as their own message, with the label), and on
  the post box page as "Sent to N connections" with the time.
- **Replies** are ordinary chat messages in that conversation. A reply goes only to the author.
- **Limit:** each member may send at most **5 posts in any 24 hours**. The per-minute message limit of #29 does not apply to the
  delivery of one post to many people (a post to 200 connections must not fail halfway); the posts limit is what protects people from flooding.
- **All or nothing:** the post and all its deliveries are saved in one step. If anything fails, nobody receives anything and the
  author can try again.
- **No connections, no post:** with no connections the box is not shown; asking the server to post anyway is refused.
- **No editing or deleting** a post in this version (a typo cannot be recalled; the author writes a follow-up). Posts are deleted when an account is deleted.
- Disconnecting or blocking works as before (#28, #29): the post stays in the stored conversation but the conversation is hidden.
- Admins cannot read posts or messages.

## What a member sees

- **`/messages`** gets a box at the top, **Post to my connections**: a text box (2,000 characters, counter near the limit), a line
  "This will be sent as a message to N connections", and a **Post** button. After posting it says "Sent to N connections" and clears the box.
  When the limit is reached it says so and shows when posting is possible again.
- **Under the box:** "Your recent posts": up to the last 10, each with its first lines, time and "Sent to N connections".
- **In a conversation:** a small **Post** label on messages that came from a post.
- Nothing changes on profiles.

## Database

- New table **`posts`**: `id`, `author_id` (→ `profiles`, delete cascade), `body`, `created_at`, `recipient_count`. Closed like the other
  tables (no direct read or write).
- `messages` gets a nullable **`post_id`** (→ `posts`, delete cascade), set on messages that come from a post. Existing rows stay null.
- `deliver_message` gets one more argument, `p_rate_limited boolean`, true for normal messages (unchanged behaviour) and false for post
  deliveries. It stays internal. The old three-argument version is replaced; `send_message` keeps working the same.
- New functions (members only; `security definer`, `set search_path = ''`, execute for `authenticated` only, explicit grants):
  - `create_post(p_body)`: refuses without a session, with no connections, over the 5 per 24 hours limit, or with a bad body; in one
    transaction inserts the post and one message per connected member (through `deliver_message`, not rate limited), sets `recipient_count`,
    returns the post id and count.
  - `my_posts(p_limit)`: the caller's own posts, newest first, at most 10.
  - `post_status()`: how many posts the caller has left in the window, when the oldest expires, and the number of connections (for the box).
  - `conversation_messages` also returns whether a message came from a post.
- One timestamped migration, tests included. All new objects get explicit grants and a production-like grant test.

## Testing (tests first)

- **Database:** a post reaches exactly the connected members (pending, declined, blocked and strangers get nothing) and each message links
  to the post; the count is right; the author sees their own copy in each conversation; a post with no connections is refused; the 6th post in
  24 hours is refused and one 24 hours later works; a post to 40 connections is delivered in full although the per-minute message limit is 30;
  empty, spaces-only and 2,001-character bodies are refused; a failure part-way leaves nothing behind (no post row, no messages); someone who
  connects after the post does not get it; unread counts rise for recipients; replies stay between the two; `send_message` still enforces its
  per-minute limit; anon, non-members and strangers get nothing; tables closed; production-like grant check; deleting the author deletes the post and its messages.
- **Unit:** the post form (length, trimming), the action (bad form refused, logged-out sent to login, messages for no connections / limit /
  invalid, no database text, success reports the count), the **Post** label rendering, the box text with the count and the limit message.
- **Browser QA (local, three accounts):** A posts; B and C (connected) get it unread with the label; D (not connected) does not; B replies and
  only A sees it; the box hides with no connections; the limit message after 5 posts; 375px.
- **Live:** with real accounts after release.
